Pagination

Moves between pages of a long list. Not used in v1, where the list scrolls.

Overview

Pagination splits a long list into pages and lets people move between them.

Not used in v1. The list holds at most 500 tasks (L2-003) and loads in one response, so it scrolls instead of paging. Documented for future archives and tables (D22).

  • Use numbered pagination when people need to reach a specific page; simple previous/next when order matters more than position.
  • Do not paginate the task list: rows move between filters and pages would reshuffle.

Anatomy

  1. Previous / nextChevron links with visually hidden names; disabled at the ends.
  2. Current pagearia-current="page"; accent fill.
  3. Gap"…" between non-adjacent pages, hidden from screen readers.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below SM, show only previous, the current page, next and the last page; hide the summary or move it above.
  • The list wraps rather than scrolls.

Theming

Reads --color-fg-default, --color-bg-subtle, --color-accent, --color-fg-on-accent and --color-fg-subtle.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <nav aria-label="Pagination"> landmark with a list of links; the current page link has aria-current="page". There is no APG widget pattern: these are ordinary links.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through the page links; disabled links are skipped (pointer-events: none, and remove href in production).
EnterGoes to the page and moves focus to the list's heading.

Focus

Shared ring around each 44px link. After navigation, focus moves to the top of the new results, not back to the pager.

Labelling

Numbers have aria-label="Page 3"; previous/next have visually hidden text.

Content

  • Ranges use an en dash and the item noun: "41–60 of 200 tasks".
  • Use "Previous" and "Next", not "Back" and "More".
