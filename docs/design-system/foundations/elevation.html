Foundation · Elevation

Elevation

Four shadow levels, two focus halos and nine z-layers: the card floats on the canvas and the toast floats above everything.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSource: tokens/tokens.css

Overview

Tasks uses little elevation: the card floats on the canvas, the selected filter sits on its track, and the toast floats above everything. Four shadow levels and two focus halos cover it. Shadows are tinted with ink (--shadow-color: 16 34 47) in light and pure black in dark; the mocks had raw shadow colours, including a light-theme tint reused in dark (D8).

Shadow levels

Light theme left, dark theme right. In dark, shadows are stronger and the surface itself gets lighter as it rises.

Surface hierarchy

Canvas < surface < raised, with sunken wells inside a surface. In the light theme surface and raised are both white and the shadow separates them; in the dark theme raised (--palette-ink-850) is lighter than surface, because shadows barely show on dark backgrounds. That is why the selected filter pill uses --color-bg-surface-raised: in the mocks it was the surface colour on a sunken track, 1.05:1 in dark (D9).

Focus halos

Text inputs show focus with an accent border and a 3px halo (--shadow-focus) instead of the outline ring; invalid inputs keep a danger halo (--shadow-focus-invalid). The border carries the ≥ 3:1 indicator; the halo is reinforcement.

Z-order

Nine layers. A component never writes a raw z-index.

  1. --z-base: Page content
  2. --z-raised: A focused input inside an input group, so its ring sits above neighbours
  3. --z-sticky: Sticky table headers
  4. --z-dropdown: Menus
  5. --z-overlay: Overlays behind a dialog
  6. --z-modal: Dialogs (not used in v1)
  7. --z-popover: Popovers
  8. --z-toast: The toast region (was a raw 10, D20)
  9. --z-tooltip: Tooltips and the skip link: always on top

Values are in the token table; the gaps between layers leave room to insert one without renumbering.

Usage

  • Pick the level by what the element does, not how important it looks: cards 2, pills 1, menus 3, toasts and dialogs 4.
  • Never stack two shadowed surfaces of the same level; nest a sunken well instead.
  • In dark, rely on the surface colour first and the shadow second.
  • Shadows never animate on a frame loop; the card's hover lift is a transform (L2-024.3).

Accessibility

  • Elevation is never the only way to tell surfaces apart: the selected filter is also bold and aria-pressed; cards have a distinct background from the canvas.
  • Toasts on --z-toast never cover focus: the column reserves --layout-safe-bottom beneath the list (WCAG 2.4.11).
  • In forced-colors mode shadows disappear; cards and inputs keep borders through system colours.
