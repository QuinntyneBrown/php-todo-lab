A hairline that separates groups on one surface.

Overview

A divider is a hairline that separates groups on one surface. In the product, hairlines separate the composer, the toolbar and every task row inside the card; they are part of those components (border-bottom), so the standalone .divider is for content that has no component of its own.

  • Use a divider between groups of unlike content on one surface, or with a label ("or") between alternatives.
  • Do not add a divider where spacing already separates things, between a heading and its content, or at the edge of a card.

Anatomy

  1. Rule1px (--border-width-hairline) in --color-border-default.
  2. Label (optional)--text-overline, uppercase, --color-fg-subtle.

Sizes

One weight (1px hairline); the label variant uses the overline type role.

States

Dividers are not interactive and have no states. Under prefers-contrast: more, --color-border-default is raised to --color-border-strong, and under forced colours it becomes CanvasText.

Responsive behaviour

  • Horizontal dividers span their container at every width.
  • Vertical dividers sit inside a .cluster; when the cluster wraps below SM, hide them (hidden) rather than leave a stray rule at a line end.

Theming

Reads --color-border-default and --border-width-hairline; the label reads --color-fg-subtle and --text-overline. In dark the rule is --palette-ink-800.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<hr> has the implicit separator role. Non-hr dividers use role="separator" (and aria-orientation="vertical" when vertical). Purely decorative rules that come from component borders have no role at all.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable; separators here are static, not the splitter widget.

Focus

Never receives focus.

Labelling

A labelled divider's text is read as part of the flow ("or"); keep it to one word.

Contrast

Rule on card (decorative)
Label on card

The rule is decorative (WCAG 1.4.11 does not apply): the groups it separates are also separated by spacing and headings.

Motion

None.

Touch

Not a target. Do not place a divider within 24px of two small targets where it might be mistaken for a boundary.

Content

  • Labels are one lowercase connective: "or", "and". Use sentence case only when it is a group name ("Completed").
  • Do not use a divider label as a heading; use a real heading instead.

Mapping: border-bottom: 1px solid var(--line) → var(--border-width-hairline) solid var(--color-border-default). The standalone .divider classes are system additions (D22).
