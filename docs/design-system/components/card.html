Card

The raised surface that holds the task list, and the pattern for summaries and whole-card links.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableUsed in v1

Overview

  • A card is the raised surface that groups related content. The product has exactly one: the task card that holds the composer, the toolbar and the list (L2-027.3: at MD and up the list sits on a raised surface with rounded corners).
  • The product card is flush: it has no padding of its own. The composer, toolbar, rows, banner and empty state each carry their own padding, so dividers run edge to edge and hover tints reach the corners (overflow: hidden clips them to the radius).
  • Use the boxed form (.card__header, .card__body, .card__footer) for self-contained summaries. Use the interactive form when the whole card navigates, as the mock launcher tiles do; it has one real link stretched over the card.
  • Do not use a card to decorate a single line of text or to frame a form field; use an alert for messages and a divider to separate content on one surface.

Anatomy

  1. Header (optional)Title in --text-h4, optional trailing badge or action; hairline divider below.
  2. Body--space-6 padding; children 12px apart.
  3. Footer (optional)Actions aligned to the end, --space-3 apart.
  4. Surface--color-bg-surface, --radius-xl (20px), --shadow-2, clips its children.

Four variants. The flush card is the product's; the others are for summaries and navigation.

Sizes

Cards size to their content and container; padding is fixed at --space-6 and the radius at --radius-xl.

States

Static cards have no interactive states. Interactive cards respond as a whole: the shadow lifts to --shadow-3 and, on pointer devices, the card rises 2px. Focus lands on the single link inside and the card draws the ring around itself (:focus-within).

  • In dark the surface (--palette-ink-900) sits one step above the canvas (--palette-ink-950) and the shadow deepens, so the card still reads as raised (D5).
  • There is no disabled card. Remove the link (and the card--interactive class) instead.

Responsive behaviour

  • XS (< 576px): the task card fills the 16px-gutter column; the composer button collapses to its icon (see composer).
  • SM (≥ 576px): the column is capped at 560px (--layout-container-max) and centred.
  • MD and up (≥ 768px): the column is 640px; the card keeps --radius-xl and --shadow-2 (L2-027.3). LG adds the 8vh top offset; the card never stretches wider (L2-027.4).
  • Grids of interactive cards use .grid--auto (minmax(18rem, 1fr)), so they go from one column on phones to as many as fit.
  • The lift on hover is applied only under (hover: hover); touch devices get the shadow change on press only.

Theming

The card reads --color-bg-surface, --radius-xl and --shadow-2; interactive cards add --shadow-3, selected cards --color-accent-subtle and an inset --color-accent edge. Themes change only those semantic tokens; the shadow tokens are redefined per theme (darker, stronger in dark, D8).

/* A sunken summary card for a hypothetical settings screen */
.card--sunken { background: var(--color-bg-surface-sunken); box-shadow: none; }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A card is a grouping, not a widget: use <section aria-label> when it is a landmark-worthy region (the task card is aria-label="Tasks"), <article> for a self-contained item, or a plain <div>. An interactive card contains exactly one link or button whose ::after covers the card; the card itself is never focusable and never has a click handler.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the card's single link, then to any separate buttons inside it in DOM order.
EnterFollows the link.

Focus

The link suppresses its own outline; the card draws the shared 2px --color-focus-ring at a 2px offset with :focus-within, so the ring follows the visible shape.

Labelling

The link text is the card's name, so a screen reader list of links reads "Loaded", not "Read more". Supporting text follows the link in DOM order. Secondary buttons inside an interactive card sit above the stretched link (position: relative) and need their own names.

Focus ring vs page

The card edge is defined by the shadow and the canvas tint, which is decorative: no information depends on seeing it.

Motion

Shadow and lift transition over --duration-fast; under reduced motion the change is instant.

Touch

The whole interactive card is the target, far above 44 × 44px. Nested buttons keep their own 44px targets and 8px spacing.

Content

  • Card titles are nouns or short noun phrases in sentence case: "Today", "Loaded". No trailing punctuation.
  • Supporting text is one sentence that says what is inside, as the launcher tiles do: "Five tasks, newest first, two done".
  • Never "Click here" or "Learn more" as the card link; the title is the link.
